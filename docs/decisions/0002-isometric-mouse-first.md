# ADR 0002 — Isometric, mouse-first interaction
Date: 2026-09-12. Status: accepted (explicit owner direction).

The owner confirmed a Diablo-inspired isometric interface and clicking as the key movement and fighting mechanic. Use an elevated orthographic view, perimeter HUD and central gameplay. Primary button clicks on ground move; clicks on an object / enemy approach and interact or fight when in range. Keyboard movement and E remain optional shortcuts. Do not drift toward a first-person game or a quiz-navigation interface.

The first slice implements target picking and approach for shrines, cache and guardian focus encounters. Conventional melee/bow click combat needs an additional encounter system; do not describe puzzle phase progression as a complete Diablo-style combat implementation.
